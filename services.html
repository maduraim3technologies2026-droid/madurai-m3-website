9. Google Play Store Publishing Assistance

Store Approval Guarantee

Google Play Console regulations have become increasingly rigorous. For personal developer accounts created after November 2023, Google mandates that at least 20 testers be opted into a closed test for 14 continuous days before production access can even be requested.

Our Publishing Checklist

  • Coordination of 14-day 20-tester closed testing tracks with active feedback collection
  • Accurate Data Safety declaration drafting (identifying all SDKs, ads, and telemetry)
  • Target SDK compliance (Android 14/15 minimum requirements)
  • App bundle (.aab) generation, Play App Signing, and Proguard obfuscation
  • Privacy policy hosting and URL linking directly compliant with Google Developer policies
