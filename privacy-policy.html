Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • MADURAI M3 TECHNOLOGIES (maduraim3technologies.com)

1. Introduction & Ownership

MADURAI M3 TECHNOLOGIES (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a Sole Proprietorship IT engineering and software development company based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India. We operate the website maduraim3technologies.com and provide software development, mobile application development, UI/UX design, and IT consulting services.

We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, privacy, and integrity of personal information and proprietary business data entrusted to us by visitors, prospective clients, and partners.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide high-quality software engineering services and respond to commercial project inquiries:

  • Direct Consultation Information: When you complete our contact forms or message us via email or WhatsApp, we collect your name, business email address, phone number, company name, and project brief description.
  • Technical Interaction Data: We may collect standard web server telemetry, including browser type, operating system, referring URL, IP address, and pages visited, to optimize performance and prevent malicious automated abuse.
  • Client Development Data: In the course of executing software contracts, clients may provide API credentials, database schemas, test data, and brand assets. This data is handled under strict confidentiality protocols.

3. Client Intellectual Property & Source Code Privacy

Core Confidentiality Guarantee: MADURAI M3 TECHNOLOGIES treats all client proprietary information, product concepts, source code, and business models as strictly confidential. We do not sell, trade, or repurpose client codebases for third parties.

Source code repositories and cloud environments configured for client deliverables are maintained in isolated private accounts with multi-factor authentication (MFA) and transferred completely to client ownership upon milestone settlement.

4. Mobile Applications & Google Play Store Compliance

When engineering mobile applications for publication on Google Play Store or Apple App Store, we adhere strictly to the respective platform developer policies:

  • We assist clients in formulating truthful, comprehensive Data Safety declarations reflecting the exact permissions, sensors, and SDKs embedded within their applications.
  • We design apps to adhere to the principle of data minimization—only requesting runtime permissions (e.g., Camera, Location, Storage) when functionally essential.
  • Client applications distributed to end users must maintain their own dedicated end-user privacy policies, which we assist in structuring and linking on app store listings.

5. How We Use Collected Information

We process information exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To formulate tailored technical proposals, architectural scoping estimates, and commercial quotations.
  • To communicate project milestones, staging release builds, and ongoing SLA maintenance updates.
  • To ensure website performance, security, and defense against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws, tax regulations, and statutory reporting requirements.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ modern industry safeguards to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of data. Communications through maduraim3technologies.com are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).

Inquiry data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy the commercial purpose or comply with accounting and contractual records under Indian law.

7. Indian IT Act & Statutory Rights

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India, you retain the right to:

  • Request confirmation and summary of personal data held about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate personal data.
  • Request deletion of your inquiry details, subject to contractual and legal retention duties.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our primary website does not employ intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers. We utilize only essential session cookies and performance telemetry necessary to deliver fast, secure page loads via our Edge CDN infrastructure.
